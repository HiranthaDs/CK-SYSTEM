Financial Overview

Accounting Engine & Brain: Assets, Liabilities & Equity are cumulative balances — all years up to the end of the selected fiscal year (an asset bought last year is still an asset). Revenue, Expenses & Net Profit belong to the selected year only; prior years' profits carry into Equity as Retained Earnings. Your explicit category always wins; if a category is unrecognized, the engine safely classifies by Account Name. The Trial Balance and Accounting Equation (A = L + E) are verified continuously. The manufacturing chain is controlled as Bulk Raw Material → Converted Chips → Finished Goods → Sales. Conversion wages are accrued to Piecework Wages Payable and cleared by payroll, preventing double counting. Production overhead credits are treated as payable/clearing liabilities, never as income or expense reductions.
