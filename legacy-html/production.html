1. Receive unloaded/bulk plastic into Bulk RM Stock.
2. Use Chip Conversion to break PP, HI or other plastic into chips, allocate employees and accrue piecework wages by quantity/rate.
3. Production can consume converted chip stock only. Bulk/unloaded plastic can never be selected directly for production.
4. The backend re-checks stock and cost before posting balanced inventory and payroll ledgers.
